# Minimal logo brief for Figma

Based on `README.md`, `docs/SPEC.md`, and `src/app/globals.css`.

Closer helps hospitalized children stay connected with family, friends and their care team
through small everyday moments: a check-in, a hug, a card or a conversation. The brand should
feel warm, reassuring and welcoming to children and adults. Its existing UI uses rounded
shapes, Nunito headings, accessible body text, a warm ivory background and soft orange accents.

## Paste into Figma

Design a minimalist vector logo for **Closer**, an app that helps hospitalized children feel
connected to their loved ones through small everyday moments.

Explore three simple symbols built from two rounded forms gently moving toward one another.
Let the negative space suggest closeness, a hug or a small shared spark. Keep the geometry
distinctive and balanced, with very few shapes and clear recognition at 24 pixels. The feeling
is quiet warmth, belonging and reassurance. It should welcome both a nine-year-old child and
their parent.

Pair the strongest symbol with the word **Closer** in a soft, rounded, highly legible wordmark;
use Nunito as the starting point and refine spacing. Use the existing warm orange
`oklch(0.66 0.17 40)` with warm ivory `oklch(0.985 0.012 75)` and deep warm brown
`oklch(0.25 0.03 50)`. Also show a single-color version in dark ink and reversed white.

Use flat vector shapes with generous space. Avoid medical crosses, hospital imagery,
brain diagrams, robot or AI motifs, gradients, shadows, tiny details, stock heart icons and
infantile cartoon mascots. The logo represents human connection; it should not imply a
medical diagnosis or monitoring service.

Create editable Figma vectors and organized frames containing the three concepts, the chosen
horizontal symbol-and-wordmark lockup, the symbol alone, a square app-icon treatment, and
24 px / 48 px / 128 px size checks. Include clear-space and minimum-size guidance. Present
everything on a clean ivory canvas with short concept notes.
